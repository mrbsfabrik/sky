How it works. Aircraft broadcast their position by ADS-B; volunteers' ground receivers upload it to community feeds (adsb.lol, with airplanes.live and adsb.fi as backups). This page asks the feed for everything within about 46 miles, then works out distance, bearing, elevation angle and closest approach itself. Airline routes come from scheduled-route data and can differ from what a given flight is actually doing. Some aircraft are missing: no transponder, privacy-blocked registrations, and some military flights. Not for navigation. The map centre is the middle of ZIP 30542, not your street — set exact coordinates under ⚙.
